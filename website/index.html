How it works

From scrolling to knowing what you want.

Furniture shopping means hundreds of tabs and endless filters. Arvelle turns it into a few relaxed minutes of yes and no.

01

Pick a room

Start with your bedroom, living room or kitchen, or go straight to sofas, rugs or bar stools.

02

Swipe what you love

Right to keep, left to skip. With every swipe, Arvelle learns your style, materials and price range, and shows you more of what fits.

03

Buy from the store

Group favorites into lists for each room. When you’re ready, tap Buy now and finish on the retailer’s own website.

For retailers & how we make money

Shoppers who already love it.

Every shopper Arvelle sends you has already chosen your product over the alternatives. They tap Buy now on a piece they kept, and land on your product page ready to purchase.

Affiliate disclosure. Arvelle is free to use. When you tap Buy now and make a purchase, the retailer may pay Arvelle a small commission through its affiliate program. This never changes the price you pay.

Commissions don’t decide what you see: Arvelle ranks products by your own taste. We only work with established retailers, and you always complete your purchase on the retailer’s own website.

Retail and partnership enquiries: hello@arvelleco.com

Questions

Good to know.

Is Arvelle free?

Yes. Arvelle is free to download and use. We earn a small commission from retailers on some purchases, at no extra cost to you.

Do I buy through Arvelle?

No. When you tap Buy now, you go to the retailer’s own website, where you check out, pay and arrange delivery exactly as you normally would.

When can I try it?

Arvelle is in testing now and launching soon on iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you once, when it’s ready.

Do I need an account?

No. You can swipe and save without one. Sign in with Apple or Google if you want your favorites and taste saved across devices.

What do you do with my data?

We use your swipes only to personalize your recommendations, and we never sell your personal information. Read our privacy policy.
