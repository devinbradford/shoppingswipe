# Image-permission follow-up requests (short versions)

**Status:** Awin gave **verbal** approval by phone. That is not written approval, and the P1–P4 scope isn't documented. Retailer-image work stays paused until written replies are recorded in `IMAGE_PERMISSIONS.md` (A2, A3, …). The longer versions and the background are in that file.

## 1. To Awin (publisher support or your account contact)

> Subject: Confirming our call: product-image analysis, publisher 3147062 (CureAvo)
>
> Hi [name], thanks for the call on [date]. For our records, could you confirm in writing whether the following uses of product images from the Tribesigns (92307), Sicotas (110532) and Welax/HONSIT (108888) feeds are permitted, and any conditions?
>
> - **P1:** showing the photos (loaded from the advertisers' own image servers) to a private panel of 3–4 people who rate visual similarity;
> - **P2:** temporary copies of at most 600 small images (≤512 px) on our own computers, deleted after processing, and never published or sent to third-party AI services;
> - **P3:** computing numeric "embeddings", duplicate fingerprints and style attributes from them, with open-source models we run ourselves;
> - **P4:** keeping only that derived data (no images) while the products are listed, to recommend them inside CureAvo through our Awin links, and deleting it within 30 days after a product or program ends, or on request.
>
> Does your approval cover the advertisers' own rights in these images, or should we ask each advertiser?
>
> Thanks, Devin

## 3. What to check yourself in Awin (about 10 minutes)

For each of the three advertisers, open **Advertisers → [brand] → Terms** and copy any sentence about:
- product data or feeds;
- images or creative;
- modification or derivative works;
- scraping;
- AI or machine learning;
- deletion on leaving the program.

Paste them to Claude; they decide whether P1–P4 need the advertiser's explicit yes.
